I am a PhD student at the [Institute for Research in Technology (IIT)](https://www.iit.comillas.edu/) at Universidad Pontificia Comillas (Madrid). My research focuses on **microeconomics and environmental, energy & transport economics**, working at the intersection of economic theory and sustainability policy.

My current research examines how individuals and firms respond to pricing mechanisms in the energy and transport sectors. This includes work on congestion pricing in highway corridors, electricity market reform, and the distributional effects of climate and energy policies.

I have also contributed to policy-oriented research on the Iberian electricity market and on strategies for a fair and efficient decarbonisation of the Spanish economy.
